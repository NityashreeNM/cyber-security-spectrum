# Cybersecurity Fundamentals

This section establishes the foundational knowledge required to understand, assess, design, and improve security across IT, cloud, applications, networks, IoT, and operational technology environments.

It covers core security principles, risk management, threats, vulnerabilities, controls, governance, and the lifecycle of a secure system. These concepts provide the foundation for the more advanced domains in **Cyber Security Spectrum**.

## Learning objectives

After completing this section, you should be able to:

- Explain cybersecurity and distinguish it from information security, IT security, privacy, safety, and resilience.
- Describe confidentiality, integrity, availability, authenticity, accountability, and non-repudiation.
- Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities, attack surfaces, and risks.
- Understand basic risk assessment and risk treatment methods.
- Explain the purpose of security policies, standards, procedures, and governance.
- Recognize the importance of people, processes, and technology.
- Apply security principles throughout the system lifecycle.
- Use foundational concepts when studying networking, Linux, cloud, applications, and incident response.

## Completion criteria

You should be able to complete this section when you can:

- Explain foundational cybersecurity concepts clearly.
- Distinguish cybersecurity from related disciplines.
- Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and consequences.
- Analyze a simple scenario using risk and security principles.
- Convert a security objective into a requirement and control.
- Identify evidence that demonstrates whether a control is effective.
